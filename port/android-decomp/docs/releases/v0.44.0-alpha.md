# CubeEvil4 v0.44.0 (Alpha)

> **⚠️ Early alpha for testers.** Expect bugs and keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Memory card →
> Export Savegames).

## Fixed

- No more crash at the elevator late on the island once it arrives (after the scene with Saddler).
- On Mali GPUs, one of the app's shaders no longer fails to build (it only showed up in the log).
- Movies no longer show green and wrong colours on some Adreno GPUs (Galaxy A36).

## New

- "60 FPS (experimental)" in the app menu: in-between pictures make movement smoother. The game itself
  still runs at 30; it needs about twice the GPU power and adds a little delay.
- Picture format "4:3 (no bars)": on 4:3 screens the picture fills the screen while you play;
  cutscenes and menus keep their bars.
- Touch in the inventory: tap an item to select it, tap it again for its menu; tap an entry, tap it
  again to use it. Tap the tabs at the top or swipe left or right to change them.
- Knife by swipe: a quick swipe across the picture draws the knife and slashes; swipe up or down to
  slash high or low (switch in the app menu under Comfort Controls).

## Changed

- The error log names each enemy's list entry and flags, and dynamite thrown, lit or exploded.

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.44.0-alpha.apk` over v0.43.0; discs, saves and settings stay. New here? See
the [README](https://github.com/Andiweli/CubeEvil4-Android#readme) for requirements and first
steps.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Error log, on by default) and send the
report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Error log → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.
