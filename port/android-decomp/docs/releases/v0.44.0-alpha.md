# CubeEvil4 v0.44.0 (Alpha)

> **⚠️ Early alpha for testers.** Expect bugs and keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Memory card →
> Export Savegames).

## Fixed

- No more crash at the elevator on the island after the scene with Saddler.
- One of the app's shaders no longer fails to build on Mali GPUs.
- Movies no longer show green, wrong colours on some Adreno GPUs (Galaxy A36).

## New

- "60 FPS (experimental)" in the app menu: in-between pictures make movement smoother; it needs
  about twice the GPU power, so turn MSAA off or the resolution down a step.
- Picture format "4:3": while you play, the picture fills the screen's height without the game's
  bars, on 4:3 screens the whole screen ("4:3 (GC)" keeps them as on the GameCube).
- Touch in the inventory: tap an item (attache case, key items, treasures) to select it and again
  for its menu, tap a file to read it, swipe left or right to change the tab.
- Knife by swipe: a quick swipe draws the knife and slashes, swiping up or down slashes high or low
  (switch in the app menu under Comfort Controls).

## Changed

- With 60 FPS on, the FPS display also shows the pictures on the screen.
- The error log names each enemy's list entry and flags, and dynamite thrown, lit or exploded.

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.44.0-alpha.apk` over v0.43.0; discs, saves and settings stay. New here? See
the [README](https://github.com/Andiweli/CubeEvil4-Android#readme) for requirements and first
steps.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Error log, on by default) and send the
report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Error log → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.
