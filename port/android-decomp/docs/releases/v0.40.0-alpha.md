# CubeEvil4 v0.40.0 (Alpha)

> **⚠️ Work in progress.** This is an early alpha for testers. Expect crashes, graphics errors and
> other bugs, and keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Memory card → Export Savegames). The game
> is the debug build of 2004; use its debug menu with care.

The game now starts on **32-bit-only devices with Android 14** as well, the game's **Dolby Pro
Logic II** sound can play as surround through Android's spatial audio, and the sound comes with
less delay. Thank you for testing and for your reports!

The version numbers are simpler from now on: each new alpha raises the middle number (this one is
0.40.0), a quick fix in between the last one (for example 0.40.1).

## Fixed

- **32-bit-only devices with Android 14 did not start the game** ("core not started: MEM1 … is
  taken in this process (by … dalvik-main space)"). On these devices Android keeps its own memory
  exactly where the game needs its memory. The game now notices this and runs in a process of
  its own next to the app, where that memory is free. Everything else stays the same: on all
  other devices the game runs as before. If this separate game process ever stops, the app tells
  you ("The game core stopped") and offers to send the error report.

## New

- **Dolby Pro Logic II surround:** choose "Dolby Pro Logic II" under AUDIO SETUP in the game's
  own options. On devices with Android's **spatial audio**
  (Android 12L and later, for example with suitable headphones) the game's front and rear
  channels then play as surround. Without spatial audio the game sounds as before (stereo).
  The game's reverb in this mode also used a wrong rear channel; fixed.

## Improved

- **Less sound delay:** the app now keeps only as much sound in Android's buffer as your device
  needs, instead of filling the whole buffer. This is most noticeable with Bluetooth headphones
  (Bluetooth itself still adds its own delay). The first time on a new output (speaker, wired or
  Bluetooth headphones) you may hear a few very short gaps while the app learns the right size;
  it remembers it.
- **Smaller download:** the APK is about half as big. Android unpacks the game's libraries while
  installing, which takes a little longer once.

## Update from v0.38.3

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.40.0-alpha.apk` over v0.38.3 (Alpha 4). Your imported discs and your saves
stay.

## Known issues

- **32-bit-only devices with Android 14:** the separate game process is new. Please report how it
  runs on your device (speed, sound, crashes) with the error report. Devices with 2 GB of memory
  and older graphics chips may be slow; lower the resolution and switch anti-aliasing off there.
- **Short stutters the first time a scene appears.** The graphics programs (shaders) for each new
  scene are prepared once on your device and then kept; after that the same scene runs smoothly.
- **Anti-aliasing (MSAA)** costs a lot of speed on many devices: 4× MSAA at a high resolution
  can drop below 30 pictures a second. Use 2× or Off if the game feels slow.
- Everything listed under "Still in testing" below.

## What you need

- Android 6.0 or later, OpenGL ES 3.0, ARM 32- or 64-bit (one APK for both)
- A game controller (built in, USB or Bluetooth) or the touch screen
- Your own disc images (ISO or GCM) of **both** discs of the GameCube debug build from
  November 25, 2004 (`G4BE08`). No game data is included.

## First install

1. Download `CubeEvil4-v0.40.0-alpha.apk` below and install it (allow installing from this
   source when Android asks).
2. Open **CubeEvil4**, choose **Import Disc 1** and **Import Disc 2**, then **Start game**.

## What works

- Title screen, menus and the opening movie with sound
- The police car ride and the village of chapter 1: walking, aiming, shooting, enemies, map and
  inventory
- Saving at the typewriter and loading from the title screen, on 32- and 64-bit devices; memory
  card import and export as `.gci` (Dolphin and GameCube)
- Sound and music through the game's own sound code, stereo or Dolby Pro Logic II surround; the
  movies of both discs
- Controller like the GameCube pad, with vibration, or the alternate layout with right-stick
  aiming; touch controls; gyro aiming
- App menu (SELECT): resolution, anti-aliasing, texture filter, 16:9 or 4:3, FPS display, depth
  of field, controls, memory card, error report

## Still in testing

- Testers have played up to the cabin in chapter 2; later chapters have not been played through
  yet and may crash or show graphics errors
- The separate game process on a real 32-bit-only device with Android 14 (on our test device it
  runs with 30 pictures a second and clean sound when we switch it on for testing)
- Dolby Pro Logic II surround on a device with spatial audio (our test device has none)
- Devices and graphics chips other than the two tested so far (Retroid Pocket Flip 2 with
  Android 13, POCO X7 Pro with Android 16), and speed on weaker devices

## Not yet

- The retail version of the game (only the debug build of November 25, 2004 is supported)
