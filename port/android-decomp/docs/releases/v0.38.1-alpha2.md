# CubeEvil4 v0.38.1 – Alpha 2

> **⚠️ Work in progress.** This is an early alpha for testers. Expect crashes, graphics errors and
> other bugs, and keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Memory card → Export). The game is the
> debug build of 2004; use its debug menu with care.

A bug-fix update of Alpha 1, from the first tester reports. Thank you for testing!

## Fixed

- **Crash on 64-bit devices when shooting objects that break apart** (splinters and debris flying
  off a hit, seen in the village).
- **Missing enemies on 64-bit devices:** in some rooms the game ran out of memory while loading an
  enemy and left it out. 64-bit devices now get the extra memory the game needs there.
- **Wrong light changes on 64-bit devices:** some lights that change their brightness over time
  followed the wrong brightness pattern (for example, one stayed at full brightness).

## Update from v0.38.0

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.38.1-alpha2.apk` over v0.38.0. Your imported discs and your saves stay.

## Known issues

- **Short stutters the first time a scene appears.** The graphics programs (shaders) for each new
  scene are prepared once on your device and then kept; after that the same scene runs smoothly.
  A fix that prepares them in advance is planned.
- Everything listed under "Still in testing" below.

## What you need

- Android 6.0 or later, OpenGL ES 3.0, ARM 32- or 64-bit (one APK for both)
- A game controller (built in, USB or Bluetooth); no touch controls
- Your own disc images (ISO or GCM) of **both** discs of the GameCube debug build from
  November 25, 2004 (`G4BE08`). No game data is included.

## First install

1. Download `CubeEvil4-v0.38.1-alpha2.apk` below and install it (allow installing from this
   source when Android asks).
2. Open **CubeEvil4**, choose **Import Disc 1** and **Import Disc 2**, then **Start game**.

## What works

- Title screen, menus and the opening movie with sound
- The police car ride and the village of chapter 1: walking, aiming, shooting, enemies, map and
  inventory
- Saving at the typewriter and loading from the title screen, on 32- and 64-bit devices; memory
  card import and export as `.gci` (Dolphin and GameCube)
- Sound and music through the game's own sound code; the movies of both discs
- Controller like the GameCube pad, with vibration
- App menu (SELECT): resolution (1080p and above), anti-aliasing, texture filter, 16:9 or 4:3,
  FPS display, depth of field, error report

## Still in testing

- Later chapters have not been played through yet and may crash or show graphics errors
- Devices and graphics chips other than the two tested so far (Retroid Pocket Flip 2 with
  Android 13, POCO X7 Pro with Android 16), and speed on weaker devices

## Not yet

- Touch controls
- The retail version of the game (only the debug build of November 25, 2004 is supported)

## Found a bug?

Open the app menu (SELECT) → Error log → **Send report** and tell us what you did, on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.
