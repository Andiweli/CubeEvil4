# CubeEvil4 v0.41.0 (Alpha)

> **⚠️ Early alpha for testers.** Expect bugs and keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Memory card →
> Export Savegames).

## Fixed

- No more crash when a Ganado grabs Luis in the cabin (chapter 2-3, 64-bit devices).
- Luis says his line again after being freed from a grab.
- Touch controls no longer stay hidden on phones that report a controller that isn't there
  (e.g. Redmi 15 5G): tap the screen to bring them back.

## New

- **Touch Controls: Auto, Force or Suppress** (app menu). Suppress hides them while a controller
  is connected, also when you touch the screen (e.g. for screenshots).
- The app menu follows your phone's language (English, German so far).

## Changed

- App menu: "Latch L and R" moved to Touch Controls; gyro aiming moved to the new "Comfort
  Controls" row.

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.41.0-alpha.apk` over v0.40.0; discs, saves and settings stay. New here? See
the [README](https://github.com/Andiweli/CubeEvil4-Android#readme) for requirements and first
steps.

**Found a bug?** App menu (SELECT) → Error log → **Send report**, on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.
