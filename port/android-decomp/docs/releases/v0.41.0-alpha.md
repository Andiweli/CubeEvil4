# CubeEvil4 v0.41.0 (Alpha)

> **⚠️ Work in progress.** This is an early alpha for testers. Expect crashes, graphics errors and
> other bugs, and keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Memory card → Export Savegames). The game
> is the debug build of 2004; use its debug menu with care.

The cabin fight in chapter 2-3 no longer stops when a Ganado grabs Luis, the touch controls have a
new setting (Auto, Force, Suppress), and the app menu speaks your phone's language where there is
a translation (English and German so far). Thank you for your reports!

## Fixed

- **The game stopped when a Ganado grabbed Luis in the cabin** (chapter 2-3, during the siege,
  a while after Luis' first shot; on 64-bit devices). The game hands Luis the grab through
  the data of its other partner, Ashley, and on 64-bit devices that data lies elsewhere. Luis now
  gets the grab directly, as on the GameCube. The siege now plays through on our test device.
- **Luis' reaction line after he is freed from a grab** was missing (on all devices); he says it
  again.
- **The touch controls stayed hidden without a controller** on some phones (seen on a Redmi 15
  5G): the phone reports a controller that is not there, and the app hid the touch controls, and
  with them the app menu. Now a **tap on the screen** brings them back (setting Auto). The error
  report names each device the app counts as a controller.

## New

- **App menu → Touch Controls: Auto, Force or Suppress.**
  - **Auto** (the default): shown when no controller is connected; with a controller a tap on the
    screen shows them and the next button hides them again.
  - **Force:** always shown (this was "Use Touch Controls" before; if you had it on, it is set).
  - **Suppress:** hidden while a controller is connected, also when you touch the screen (for
    example to take screenshots). Without any controller they still appear, so you are never
    left without controls.
- **The app menu in your language:** it follows your phone's language, English where there is no
  translation yet. German is the first one; more will follow. The game's own texts stay as on the
  GameCube.

## Improved

- **App menu, Controls:** "Latch L and R" now sits under the touch settings; **Comfort Controls**
  holds Alternate Controls and gyro aiming.

## Update from v0.40.0

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.41.0-alpha.apk` over v0.40.0. Your imported discs, your saves and your
settings stay. If the game stopped in the cabin, load your last save and play the siege again.

## Known issues

- **32-bit-only devices with Android 14:** the separate game process is new since v0.40.0. Please
  report how it runs on your device (speed, sound, crashes) with the error report. Devices with
  2 GB of memory and older graphics chips may be slow; lower the resolution and switch
  anti-aliasing off there.
- **Short stutters the first time a scene appears.** The graphics programs (shaders) for each new
  scene are prepared once on your device and then kept; after that the same scene runs smoothly.
- **Anti-aliasing (MSAA)** costs a lot of speed on many devices: 4× MSAA at a high resolution
  can drop below 30 pictures a second. Use 2× or Off if the game feels slow.
- Everything listed under "Still in testing" below.

## What you need

- Android 6.0 or later, OpenGL ES 3.0, ARM 32- or 64-bit (one APK for both)
- A game controller (built in, USB or Bluetooth) or the touch screen
- Your own disc images (ISO or GCM) of **both** discs of the GameCube debug build from
  November 25, 2004 (`G4BE08`). No game data is included.

## First install

1. Download `CubeEvil4-v0.41.0-alpha.apk` below and install it (allow installing from this
   source when Android asks).
2. Open **CubeEvil4**, choose **Import Disc 1** and **Import Disc 2**, then **Start game**.

## Still in testing

- Testers have played through the cabin in chapter 2; later chapters have not been played
  through yet and may crash or show graphics errors
- Dolby Pro Logic II surround on a device with spatial audio (our test device has none)
- Devices and graphics chips other than the ones tested so far, and speed on weaker devices

Open the app menu (SELECT) → Error log → **Send report** and tell us what you did and which
version the menu shows, on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.
