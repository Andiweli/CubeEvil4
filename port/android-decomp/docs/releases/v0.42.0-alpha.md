# CubeEvil4 v0.42.0 (Alpha)

> **⚠️ Early alpha for testers.** Expect bugs and keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Memory card →
> Export Savegames).

## Fixed

- No more crashes after a while on phones with a Mali GPU (many MediaTek and Exynos phones).
- Merchant tune-ups raise the level you bought; before, they landed on another level and could
  stop reloading (weapons tuned before keep their mixed levels).
- In the insect boss's arena, enemies and effects now stop during the gas bomb scenes.
- Leon blends into the aim pose of his weapons as in the original.
- Opened shelves and boxes in the village house stay open when you come back.
- Krauser picks his next position as in the original.

## Changed

- R1 no longer opens the hidden debug editors in the item and attache case screens.

## Update

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.42.0-alpha.apk` over v0.41.3; discs, saves and settings stay. New here? See
the [README](https://github.com/Andiweli/CubeEvil4-Android#readme) for requirements and first
steps.

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Error log, on by default) and send the
report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Error log → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.
