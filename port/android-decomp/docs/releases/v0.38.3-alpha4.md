# CubeEvil4 v0.38.3 – Alpha 4

> **⚠️ Work in progress.** This is an early alpha for testers. Expect crashes, graphics errors and
> other bugs, and keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Memory card → Export Savegames). The game
> is the debug build of 2004; use its debug menu with care.

An update of Alpha 3 with fixes from your reports: no more flickering branches with
anti-aliasing, gyro aiming by turning the device, and aiming with the right stick in Alternate
Controls. Thank you for testing!

## Fixed

- **Thin things such as tree branches flickered** for a moment with anti-aliasing (MSAA) 2× or 4×,
  for example branches in front of a house. The cause was found and fixed; tested on the Retroid
  Pocket Flip 2 (Adreno 650) with 2× and 4×.

## Improved

- **Gyro aiming** (app menu → **Use Gyro-Aiming**): you now aim left and right by **turning** the
  device left and right, like a camera, as in most shooters. Before, you had to tilt it like a
  steering wheel. Up and down work as before. It still only moves the aim while you aim a gun.
- **Alternate Controls:** while you aim (L2) or hold the knife (L1), the **right stick** moves the
  aim; the left stick rests meanwhile. Gyro aiming and the right stick work together.
- **Touch controls:** L and R now work like real buttons: pressed while your finger is on them.
  Before, a tap kept them pressed until the next tap; that is still there as an option (app menu →
  **Latch L and R (Touch)**), handy to aim and fire with two thumbs.
- **FPS display** (app menu → **FPS display bottom left**): it now also shows your screen's
  refresh rate, for example "30 FPS (Game) · 60 Hz". The game itself makes 30 pictures a
  second, as on the GameCube, also on screens with 90 or 120 Hz.
- **App menu:** the app's version is shown after the title, for example "(v0.38.3-Alpha4)". Please
  name it when you report a bug.

The standard button layout stays as on the GameCube: there the left stick aims, and the right
stick does not move the aim (as in the original game).

## Update from v0.38.2

Install `CubeEvil4-v0.38.3-alpha4.apk` over v0.38.2. Your imported discs and your saves stay.

## Known issues

- **Short stutters the first time a scene appears.** The graphics programs (shaders) for each new
  scene are prepared once on your device and then kept; after that the same scene runs smoothly.
- **Anti-aliasing (MSAA)** costs a lot of speed on many devices: 4× MSAA at a high resolution
  can drop below 30 pictures a second. Use 2× or Off if the game feels slow.
- **Some 32-bit-only devices do not start the game** (seen on one with Android 14; the error
  says "core not started: MEM1 … is taken in this process (by … dalvik-main space)"). Android
  keeps its own memory there at the address the game needs. A fix needs a larger change; the
  64-bit devices tested so far start normally.
- Everything listed under "Still in testing" below.

## What you need

- Android 6.0 or later, OpenGL ES 3.0, ARM 32- or 64-bit (one APK for both)
- A game controller (built in, USB or Bluetooth) or the touch screen
- Your own disc images (ISO or GCM) of **both** discs of the GameCube debug build from
  November 25, 2004 (`G4BE08`). No game data is included.

## First install

1. Download `CubeEvil4-v0.38.3-alpha4.apk` below and install it (allow installing from this
   source when Android asks).
2. Open **CubeEvil4**, choose **Import Disc 1** and **Import Disc 2**, then **Start game**.

## What works

- Title screen, menus and the opening movie with sound
- The police car ride and the village of chapter 1: walking, aiming, shooting, enemies, map and
  inventory
- Saving at the typewriter and loading from the title screen, on 32- and 64-bit devices; memory
  card import and export as `.gci` (Dolphin and GameCube)
- Sound and music through the game's own sound code; the movies of both discs
- Controller like the GameCube pad, with vibration, or the alternate layout with right-stick
  aiming; touch controls; gyro aiming
- App menu (SELECT): resolution, anti-aliasing, texture filter, 16:9 or 4:3, FPS display, depth
  of field, controls, memory card, error report

## Still in testing

- Later chapters have not been played through yet and may crash or show graphics errors
- Devices and graphics chips other than the two tested so far (Retroid Pocket Flip 2 with
  Android 13, POCO X7 Pro with Android 16), and speed on weaker devices

## Not yet

- The retail version of the game (only the debug build of November 25, 2004 is supported)
